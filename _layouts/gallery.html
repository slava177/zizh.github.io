--- layout: default --- {% comment %} A grid of photos and short clips, each opening full-screen when clicked: the Travel and Scale Models pages. The format follows Yingsi Qin's photography page (https://yingsiqin.github.io/photography.html). The page's front matter says which data file to show (gallery: travel shows _data/travel.yml, with its files in images/travel/), how many photos go in a row on large screens (columns, 4 by default; small screens get 3, phones 2), how the photos are arranged and whether a row of years goes over the photos (years: true), each showing that year's photos only; an entry's year is the start of its file name. The arrangement is a grid of 3:2 tiles by default; arrangement: mosaic shapes the tiles like the photos (portraits two cells tall, panoramas two wide, an entry with feature: true two of each), and arrangement: wall shows the photos whole, framed, with their names beneath. The shapes come from _data/photo_sizes.yml (scripts/gallery_images.py). An entry with more photos (photos: in its data file) shows its first, and the rest in the full view. The page's own content is the line under the title. The button that flips the order is in the menu at the top (_includes/site-header.html), so it stays at hand while the photos scroll. Styles: _sass/_gallery.scss.
